What it does

A texture painter that understands avatars

Paints in 3D

Strokes are projected onto the posed model, with occlusion, backface culling and mirror painting. What you see is where the paint goes.

Five brushes

Hard, Soft, Blur, Color Blend and Eraser, with pen pressure, spacing and hardness, plus a colour picker.

Mask painter

A second tab paints the shader's mask slots, such as _EmissionMask. Red, green and blue keep to their own channel, so one texture holds three masks.

Seamless blending

Color Blend turns hard borders into smooth gradients without smearing, and mixes colours perceptually so midpoints don't go muddy.

Photoshop style layers

13 blend modes, opacity, visibility, lock transparency, merge down, fill and image import. Undo it all with Ctrl+Z.

Every UV layout

UV seams, mirrored and overlapping UVs, UV0 to UV7, and islands outside 0–1 for tiling or UV tile discard.

Safe for uploads

Editor only. Nothing is added to your avatar and materials stay untouched until you export. Work is autosaved across Play mode.

Built for real avatar UVs

The model decides where paint goes, not the UV map

Each dab is rendered into texture space and tested against the 3D surface, so layouts that break ordinary painters simply work.

Across UV seams

A stroke over a seam paints both islands, and seam padding keeps mip maps free of thin lines.

Mirrored and overlapping

Texels shared by both sides of the body take paint from whichever side is under the brush.

Several textures at once

Paint a body texture on UV0 and a Poiyomi face decal on UV2 in one stroke, with a shared layer stack.

Mask painter

Masks that add up instead of overwriting

The second tab paints the shader's mask slots, from Poiyomi's _EmissionMask to the Standard shader's _DetailMask. Each colour channel is painted on its own, so one texture can carry three masks at once.

One texture, three masks

Paint red, then green over the same spot: the red mask stays and green is added on top of it. White raises every channel, Black clears them, and alpha is never touched.

Brushes stay in their channel

Hard, Soft and Blur only move the channel you picked, and the Eraser takes it back to zero. Fill, Clear and Invert do the same to the whole mask.

See what you paint

Show Mask On Model puts the mask in place of the main texture, so you can see a mask even while the effect it drives is switched off.

Install

Three steps with the Creator Companion

  1. Add the listing

    Click Add to VCC. The Creator Companion opens and asks you to confirm.

    Add to VCC

  2. Add it to your project

    Open your avatar project with Manage Project and add VRC Texture Painter.

  3. Start painting

    In Unity, open Tools > VRC Texture Painter, pick your avatar's mesh and press Start Painting.

Add the listing manually
  1. Open the Creator Companion and go to Settings, then Packages.
  2. Click Add Repository, paste the listing URL and click Add.
  3. Confirm with I Understand. The package now shows up in every project.
