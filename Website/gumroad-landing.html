FAQ

Questions before you paint

Will it add anything to my avatar or break uploads?

No. The tool is editor only: it adds no components to your avatar and doesn't touch your materials while you paint. Nothing changes until you export or overwrite a texture.

Which shaders does it work with?

The Texture Painter works with any shader's main texture, plus Poiyomi decals. The Mask Painter lists every mask slot on the material, so Poiyomi, lilToon, Standard and other shaders all work.

Which Unity version do I need?

Unity 2022.3 with the Built-in render pipeline, the version used for VRChat avatars. It's tested on 2022.3.22f1 with Linear colour space.

Can I undo mistakes?

Yes. Every stroke and layer change is on Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y through Unity's normal undo, and you can save projects that keep every layer to keep editing later.

Does it handle mirrored UVs and UV seams?

Yes. Paint is placed by the 3D surface, not the UV map, so strokes cross seams cleanly and land on mirrored or overlapping UVs and on any UV channel.
