Texture Painter

Fix what looks wrong, where it looks wrong

You paint on the avatar itself, not on a flat UV map. Every stroke follows the 3D surface, so it keeps going across UV seams and lands on every piece of the texture that sits under the brush.

Seams and hard edges

Melt a hard line into the colours around it

Soften the edge with Blur, then run the Blend brush over it. Blend turns the border into a smooth gradient between the colours on both sides, without smearing paint along the stroke.

  • Works straight across UV seams, because both sides are next to each other on the model.
  • Perceptual colour mixing, so transitions don't go grey or muddy in the middle.
  • Seam padding keeps thin lines from showing up in mip maps.

Several textures, several UV channels

Blend the neck where two textures meet

Lots of avatars draw the face from its own texture, for example a Poiyomi decal on UV2 next to the body on UV0. Add both textures, and one Blend stroke over the neck paints both at once, so the colour step blends away.

  • Poiyomi decals show up as one-click buttons with the right UV channel.
  • The window tells you when parts of the mesh would not receive paint.
  • Mirrored, overlapping and tiled UVs just work.
  • Hard, Soft, Blur, Blend, Eraser and Gradient tools, with pen pressure and mirror painting.
  • Photoshop style layers with 13 blend modes, so fixes stay separate from the original texture.
  • Undo everything with Ctrl+Z, and save projects that keep every layer.
  • Export PNG or overwrite the source texture, with the original import settings kept.

Mask Painter

Poiyomi and lilToon masks, painted right on the model

Stop guessing where a mask lands on the UV map. Pick the mask, paint where you want the effect, and see the result live on your avatar.

Step 1

Pick any mask your shader has

The Mask Painter lists every mask slot on the material: Poiyomi's _EmissionMask, _GlitterMask or _PathingMap, lilToon's _EmissionBlendMask or _ShadowStrengthMask, and the slots of any other shader.

  • No mask yet? An empty slot starts black, and Create Texture saves a new one and assigns it.
  • Pick an Output Folder once and new masks save there without a dialog.

Step 2

Several masks in one texture

Choose R, G or B and each brush only changes that channel. Paint red and then green over the same spot and you keep both masks, which is exactly what shaders that read one mask per channel need.

  • Mirror paints the left and right side of the avatar in one stroke.
  • Show Mask On Model shows the mask on the avatar, even while the effect is turned off.
  • Fill, Clear and Invert work on the selected channel. Alpha is never touched.

Install

Three steps with the Creator Companion

  1. Add the listing

    Click Add to VCC. The Creator Companion opens and asks you to confirm.

    Add to VCC

  2. Add it to your project

    Open your avatar project with Manage Project and add VRC Texture Painter.

  3. Start fixing

    In Unity, open Tools > VRC Texture Painter, pick your avatar's mesh and press Start Painting, or switch to the Mask Painter tab.

Add the listing manually
  1. Open the Creator Companion and go to Settings, then Packages.
  2. Click Add Repository, paste the listing URL and click Add.
  3. Confirm with I Understand. The package now shows up in every project.
